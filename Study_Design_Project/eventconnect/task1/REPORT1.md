1. **Requirement Violated** — the SRS requirement ID (if any) in the **Functional Requirements** **(§2.3)** and elements that the wireframe fails to satisfy.
1. **Problem Type** — type of problems for the violated design, including inconsistent design, missing functionality, and unclear/ambiguous design.
1. **Screen & UI Element** — which and what you found it in the wireframe.
1. **Issue Description** — one or two sentences on what's wrong and why it conflicts with the requirement.
1. **User Workflow Impact** — how this affects the user, judged against the **Use Case (§2.1)** and the **User Stories (§2.2)**. Name the affected UC step or User Story (if any) and a brief note on how it affects the user's workflow.
