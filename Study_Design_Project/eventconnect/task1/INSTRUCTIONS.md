# Task 1 — Requirement Review

**Time Limit: 15-20 minutes**

---

## Background

The EventConnect team has written a short **Software Requirements Specification (SRS)** (`task1/srs.md`) and a designer has produced a low-fidelity wireframe ([Figma](https://www.figma.com/design/OeF6UuLMeyI46GlMwDd7fA/EventConnect?node-id=0-1&t=FtRi67boQdjaZkoZ-1)) of the app based on it. Before development starts, the team wants to check the wireframe against the SRS and flag anywhere they disagree.

---

## Your Role

You are a **requirements** **reviewer** doing a pre-development check via walking through the wireframe against the SRS and flag anywhere they disagree.

---

## Your Task

1. Read`task1/srs.md` for project requirements and treat this as **ground truth**, and check it against the wireframe screen by screen in the [Figma](https://www.figma.com/design/OeF6UuLMeyI46GlMwDd7fA/EventConnect?node-id=0-1&t=FtRi67boQdjaZkoZ-1) (open it in a browser).
2. For anything in the wireframe that does **not** satisfy a requirement, report it in the `task1/REPORT1.md`.
3. General opinions don't count. Please follow the **exact format** of the given example to report the **5 metrics** for each mismatch.
4. Report exactly **4** issues.

You do not need to fix anything — just report what doesn't match.

---

## Use AI To Help With Your Task

Read requirements, check wireframe elements, and ask the AI to help you check one against the other.

**Example opening prompt:**

> "I have a requirements spec and a wireframe for an app called EventConnect. Can you help me review it?"

